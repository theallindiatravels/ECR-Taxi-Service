About Balaji Travels

Welcome to Balaji Travels, your most trusted and reliable taxi service based on the beautiful East Coast Road (ECR) in Chennai. We take pride in offering premium transportation solutions to both locals and tourists visiting our vibrant city.

With years of experience in the travel industry, we understand that every journey matters. Our fleet consists of well-maintained, comfortable vehicles, and our team of professional drivers are highly experienced, polite, and deeply familiar with the routes across Chennai and South India.

24/7 Availability Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. We are always ready when you are.
Safety First Passenger safety is our top priority. All drivers are background verified.
Transparent Pricing No hidden charges or surge fees. We offer competitive rates always.
Maximum Comfort Enjoy a smooth ride in our exceptionally clean and serviced cars.

Whether you need a quick drop to the airport, a relaxing drive down the ECR, or a long outstation trip, Balaji Travels is here to serve you with dedication and care.
